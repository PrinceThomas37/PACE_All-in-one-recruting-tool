        FOR STAFFING FIRMS

        BOTH HALVES OF THE BUSINESS. ONE COIN.

        Most tools do one half: a CRM to find clients, or an ATS to fill roles. A staffing firm lives in both, every day. Walk through each one.

        SALES · WIN THE CLIENT

        STEP 1 OF 5

        FIND WHO'S HIRING

        PACE DOES

        Reads live job postings and turns each hiring company into a lead. It shows what it knows and invents nothing.

        YOU DO

        Pick the companies worth a call.

        STEP 2 OF 5

        FIND THE RIGHT PERSON

        PACE DOES

        Keeps four people to reach at each company and looks for the missing ones when you ask.

        YOU DO

        Accept the right person, or say "not this person" and they are never suggested again.

        STEP 3 OF 5

        WRITE FROM YOUR OWN MAILBOX

        PACE DOES

        Sends your sequence from your own Gmail or Outlook, within the hours and the daily number your admin sets. An AI draft is checked before it can go.

        YOU DO

        Write the sequence once, in your own words.

        STEP 4 OF 5

        REPLIES, SORTED

        PACE DOES

        Reads every reply: who wants to talk, who asked about rates, who is out of office. Anyone who asks to stop is never emailed again.

        YOU DO

        Choose where to start.

        STEP 5 OF 5

        A CLIENT, WITH JOBS

        PACE DOES

        Keeps the client, its people, its open jobs and every email in one place.

        YOU DO

        Take the job order. That is where the other half begins.
