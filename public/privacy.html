Privacy Policy

PACE · Last updated 26 October 2026

PACE is a recruiting and outreach platform. Businesses use it to manage candidates, job orders and clients, and to send and track email from their own mailboxes. This page explains what data PACE handles and why. Questions: info@paceconsultant.in.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, work email address, role and the organisation you belong to.
  • Content you add: leads, contacts, candidates, resumes, job orders, notes and email templates.
  • Email activity: the emails PACE sends for you, and whether they were delivered, opened or replied to.
  • Mailbox connection: when you connect a Google or Microsoft mailbox, the secure access token the provider gives us, and the address of that mailbox.

How we use Google user data

If you connect a Gmail mailbox, PACE asks Google for permission to:

  • Send email as you (the Gmail "send" permission), so outreach you write or approve is sent from your own address.
  • Read and organise mail in your mailbox (the Gmail "modify" permission), so PACE can detect replies to emails it sent, record them against the right lead or candidate, and move a message out of spam when you ask it to.
  • See your email address, so we know which mailbox is connected.

We use this access only to provide these features to you. For replies to outreach, PACE keeps a shortened copy of the message text (quoted history removed) so it can show you who is waiting on an answer. We do not read your mailbox for any other purpose, we do not sell Google user data, and we do not use it for advertising.

PACE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, to investigate abuse, or where the law requires.

Microsoft mailboxes

If you connect a Microsoft (Outlook) mailbox, PACE uses the equivalent permissions for the same purposes, and the same limits apply.

Who can see your data

Inside PACE, people see what they own plus what their reporting chain and organisation administrators are permitted to see. One organisation's data is kept separate from every other organisation's. We share data with service providers only where needed to run PACE (hosting and database), and with no one else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep your data while your account is active. You can disconnect a mailbox at any time inside PACE, which stops PACE's access, and you can also remove PACE's access from your Google Account permissions page. To have your data deleted, email info@paceconsultant.in and we will act on the request within 30 days.

Candidates and people we email

People contacted through PACE can opt out of further email at any time using the link in the message, and opted-out addresses are not contacted again.

Security

Access tokens and account data are stored in an access-controlled database, connections are encrypted, and each organisation's data is separated from the others.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where required, tell account owners.
